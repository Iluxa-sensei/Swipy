# Swipy – Business Plan Context (for Perplexity)

## Section A: Our Business Details

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| **Mission** | To connect founders and investors through fast, low-friction discovery and direct conversation—no middlemen—so every conversation starts from mutual interest. |
| **Vision statement** | To be the default place where people discover and connect with startups: swipe-based discovery as familiar as dating apps, with mutual matching andm in-app chat. |
| **Business Name** | Swipy |
| **Values** | Speed to first contact; direct connection (no brokers); mutual intent (match only when both sides like); design-led product; two-sided value (founders get discovered, investors discover and connect). |
| **Business Activity** | Swipe-based matchmaking platform for startups: founders add startups and get discovered; investors and peers discover, like, and match; built-in chat after mutual match. Roadmap: AI for vacancy search (partnership interest), startup promotion feature. |
| **Main product / service** | Mobile and web application: matching platform ("Tinder for startups") for startup discovery, mutual matching, and direct messaging between founders and investors or between founders. |

---

## Section B: Market Research and Target Market

### 1. Market research done
Live product feedback, founder/investor conversations, and social metrics. **300 active users** in **30+ countries**. Feedback from **LinkedIn** and in-app behaviour drove **127 commits after launch** and the **mobile version**. Discussions with **Francisco Terpolili (co-founder of Specter)** (partnership interest, suggested AI for vacancy search) and **Din Malibay (co-founder of Laminar)** (voted for Swipy on Product Hunt, suggested startup promotion feature). **15k views on socials**. Questions focused on pain points in startup discovery, interest in swipe-based flow, and chatting only after mutual interest.

### 2. Results – who will buy and why
**Who:** Founders who want to be discovered; investors who want to discover startups quickly; peers (e.g. other founders) looking for co-founders or partners.  
**Why:** Swipe-based discovery is fast and familiar. Mutual matching means both sides have shown intent (less noise). After a match, users chat in-app with no middlemen. Founders get visibility; investors and peers can filter by industry, stage, and location. Feedback from users and founders (e.g. Francisco Terpolili, Din Malibay) supports demand for the core product and for extensions (AI for vacancy search, startup promotion).

## Section 3: Main competitors and their strengths and weaknesses

| Competitor Name | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|-----------------|-----------|------------|
| AngelList / Wellfound | Large network, jobs + fundraising, brand | Heavy, form-based discovery; no swipe or instant match |
| LinkedIn | Huge user base, professional profiles | Not built for startup–investor matching; cold outreach; no mutual-match flow |
| Product Hunt | Discovery and visibility for launches | One-off launches, not ongoing matching or chat |
| Y Combinator / accelerators | Cohort model, mentorship, investor access | Application-heavy, selective; not self-serve discovery |
| Pitch decks / email | Familiar, no platform lock-in | No discovery layer, no mutual intent; cold outreach |

---

## Section 4: Our competitive advantage
Swipe-based discovery (fast, familiar UX); mutual matching (conversations only when both sides like); direct connection in one place (no brokers, no "request intro"). We focus on making first contact fast and high-signal. Early traction: 300 active users, 30+ countries, interest from Specter and Laminar co-founders, Product Hunt support; feedback suggests AI for vacancy search and startup promotion as differentiators.

## Section F: Marketing and Sales Plan – 4 P's

- **Product** – Our product/service is a mobile and web matching platform ("Tinder for startups") where founders add startups and get discovered, and investors/peers discover, like, and match; after a mutual match, users chat in-app. Filters (industry, stage, location, etc.) narrow the stack.  
- **Price** – We will sell it at this price: free tier at launch (Vercel + Supabase free); future monetisation (e.g. premium features for investors, promotion for startups) to be defined. Pricing strategy: grow usage and engagement first, then introduce paid tiers or take rate.  
- **Place** – We will sell our product/service at this place: web (desktop and mobile) at our domain; app store distribution later if needed.  
- **Promotion** – We will promote our product/service by: social content (15k views); Product Hunt launch; outreach to investors and founders (e.g. Specter, Laminar); LinkedIn and community posts; partnerships and word of mouth.
