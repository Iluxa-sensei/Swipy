# Swipy — Context for Perplexity Space

## What is Swipy?

**Swipy** is a Tinder-style platform for **startups, investors, and founders**. It replaces manual investor search, cold emailing, and lack of visibility into investor interest with **AI-powered, Tinder-style matching** and connections based on **mutual interest**.

---

## Core value proposition

- **Problem:** Founders spend time manually searching for investors, sending cold emails, and have no clear signal of investor interest.
- **Solution:** Smart, AI-powered matching and a swipe-based interface so startups and investors discover each other and connect only when there is mutual interest.

---

## Key features

- **Tinder-style swiping** — Swipe to like or pass on startups/investors.
- **AI-powered startup–investor matching** — Matching driven by AI, not only filters.
- **Personalized investor recommendations** — Tailored suggestions for founders (and vice versa).
- **Smart filtering** — Filter by industry, stage, location, and other criteria.
- **Profile analytics** — Insights on profile and engagement.
- **Pitch-deck upload** — Startups can attach or link pitch decks.
- **In-app chat** — Direct messaging after a match.
- **Insights & notifications** — Activity and match alerts.

## Roadmap (high level)

- **MVP (Month 1–2):** Gather feedback; improve UX/UI, security, backend and frontend.
- **Awareness (Month 3–4):** Attract first 300–500 users via LinkedIn, Instagram, Telegram, ProductHunt.
- **Partnership launch (Month 5–6):** Partnerships with Astana Hub, Techstars, and university clubs (e.g. NU, AITU, KBTU).
- **Paid features (Month 7–8):** Premium plan for startups and investors; referral program (e.g. invite 5 → get 1 free push).
- **Expand (Month 9–12):** Student ambassadors at global universities (Ivy, MIT), international accelerators, B2B version, next funding round.

## Ecosystem & affiliations

Swipy is part of the global startup ecosystem, with ties to programs and hubs such as **Seedstars**, **Astana Hub**, **500** (500 Startups), **Harvard Innovation Labs**, and **Y Combinator** (as indicated on the product roadmap).

---

## Tech stack

- **Backend / data:** Supabase (auth, database, realtime).
- **Frontend:** React.
- **Build / tooling:** Vite (current app); roadmap graphic also references Next.js.
- **Deployment:** Vercel.
